# CreditBridge — Statutory Scheme & Eligibility Guide

## 1. Overview of Supported Indian MSME Credit Schemes

CreditBridge maps micro-enterprise profiles directly against central government credit and subsidy schemes governed by the **Ministry of Finance (Department of Financial Services)** and the **Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME)**.

## 3. Deep Dive: Scheme Rules & Statutory Citations

### 3.1. Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)
* **Governing Body:** Department of Financial Services (DFS), Ministry of Finance / MUDRA (SIDBI subsidiary).
* **Statutory Master Circular:** `F.No. 27/01/2015-CP` / PMMY Operational Guidelines.
* **RBI Mandate:** `RBI/2020-21 FIDD.MSME & NFS.BC.No.3/06.02.31/2020-21` — Banks are mandated not to accept collateral security for loans up to ₹10 Lakhs extended to MSE units.
* **Tier Segmentation:**
  1. **Shishu:** Loans up to ₹50,000. Specifically intended for petty vendors, tailors, micro-repairs. Zero processing fee.
  2. **Kishore:** Loans above ₹50,000 and up to ₹5,00,000. Geared towards equipment purchase or working capital cycle expansion.
  3. **Tarun:** Loans above ₹5,00,000 and up to ₹10,00,000.
  4. **Tarun Plus (Budget 2024-25 Enhancement):** Enhanced ceiling up to ₹20,00,000 for entrepreneurs who previously availed and successfully repaid loans under the Tarun tier.

### 3.2. CGTMSE (Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises)
* **Governing Body:** Ministry of MSME and Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI).
* **Statutory Circular:** `CGTMSE/OPERATIONS/2023-24/104 (Circular No. 68/2023-24)`.
* **Guarantee Cover Parameters:**
  * Micro Enterprises up to ₹5 Lakhs: **85% guarantee cover**.
  * Women-Owned Enterprises: **85% guarantee cover** across credit tranches with concessional annual guarantee fee (AGF) of 0.37%.
  * Units in Aspirational Districts / North-East Region: **85% guarantee cover**.
  * General MSEs up to ₹500 Lakhs: **75% guarantee cover**.
* **Key Requirement:** Mandatory active Udyam Registration Number.

### 3.3. Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP)
* **Governing Body:** Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC), Ministry of MSME.
* **Statutory Guidelines:** `KVIC/PMEGP/Policy/2022-23/Cir-12`.
* **Greenfield Restriction:** PMEGP first-time loan assistance is **strictly restricted to new micro-enterprises**. Existing units seeking working capital must apply under MUDRA or CGTMSE.
* **Subsidy & Contribution Structure:**
  * **General Category:**
    * Urban: 15% Margin Money Subsidy | 10% Owner Contribution | 75% Bank Loan.
    * Rural: 25% Margin Money Subsidy | 10% Owner Contribution | 65% Bank Loan.
  * **Special Category (Women, SC, ST, OBC, Minorities, Aspirational Districts):**
    * Urban: 25% Margin Money Subsidy | 5% Owner Contribution | 70% Bank Loan.
    * Rural: **35% Margin Money Subsidy** | 5% Owner Contribution | 60% Bank Loan.
* **Educational Norm:** For projects > ₹10 Lakhs in manufacturing or > ₹5 Lakhs in service, minimum educational qualification is 8th standard pass.

### 3.4. Stand-Up India Scheme
* **Governing Body:** Department of Financial Services (DFS), Ministry of Finance.
* **Statutory Directive:** `DFS/F.No. 28/01/2016-CP-SUIS`.
* **Mandate:** Every commercial bank branch must extend composite credit (term loan + working capital) between ₹10 Lakhs and ₹1 Crore to at least one SC or ST borrower and at least one woman borrower.
* **Scope:** Greenfield enterprises only (manufacturing, services, trading, or agri-allied).
* **Repayment Tenor:** Up to 7 years with maximum moratorium period of 18 months.

## 4. Integration with JanSamarth Portal

**JanSamarth** ([jansamarth.in](https://www.jansamarth.in)) is the Government of India's unified portal linking central credit-linked schemes to 125+ Member Lending Institutions (MLIs).

CreditBridge acts as an **upstream readiness layer** for JanSamarth:
1. **Pre-Validation:** Eliminates file rejections by verifying cashflow debt servicing capacity and statutory category match before the entrepreneur logs into JanSamarth.
2. **Missing Document Prevention:** Identifies missing items (such as Udyam registration, DPR, or address synchronization) so the applicant arrives at JanSamarth fully prepared.
3. **Transparent Handoff:** Provides the applicant with a downloadable, rule-cited readiness summary ready for branch or portal submission.
